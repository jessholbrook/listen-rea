# Research operations home

Open **Research library → Operations home** for a workspace overview in English, Spanish or French. Existing library search remains the default section. This overview brings together current follow-up tasks, handoff review requests, recent coordination activity and accessible saved review views.

The five summary cards count overdue unfinished follow-ups, packets with at least one overdue pending reviewer, blocked packets, packets awaiting the signed-in member’s review, and packets ready for final approval. Review counts are packets, not reviewer assignments. Due today is not overdue; completed tasks and current reviewer approvals are excluded from overdue counts. Dates use the viewer’s local calendar and update on focus and every minute while open. Other sessions’ research changes require explicit refresh; the loaded timestamp records when the current overview loaded.

Overdue follow-ups, blocked packets and recent activity show at most five preview rows each. Summary counts use the complete current task and packet queues, independently of preview limits. Full-queue buttons preserve the relevant filters and chosen language. A follow-up preview opens overdue tasks for that collection. Packet links load the current original packet; a removed or replaced packet produces an unavailable message rather than opening a new generation. Recent activity is the first five events from the existing newest-first retained history page. It is not a full audit trail. Full history opens without the preview limit.

The saved-view chooser combines the viewer’s private presets and readable workspace-shared presets across inbox, workload and activity. Opening a preset fetches its current detail with the version seen in the overview, then restores its exact filters (including workload selection and absolute activity dates) in the original screen. Changed, deleted or newly private presets require refresh; the overview is cleared on failure. Relative `mine` and due-window semantics remain those of the existing screens. A reader can open readable presets without gaining mutation permissions. Manage review views opens the original inbox controls.

The overview uses the original authenticated action-center, review-inbox, review-activity and saved-view projections. All four reads must succeed before any counts or preview lists appear; failed refreshes clear previous results and show an explicit error, rather than zero or partial counts. Requests are aborted on navigation and superseded loads cannot replace newer state. These are independently validated reads, not a single database snapshot; research can change during loading. Activity rows are intersected with the loaded current packet IDs to suppress a packet pruned by another projection. Destination screens revalidate current source/generation and permissions before displaying or changing anything.

No new endpoint, persisted overview, research copies, background polling of research state, provider calls, services, environment variables, domains or email are introduced. The overview does not approve research, mark tasks complete, add user activity, send reminders or change saved presets. Existing validation may invalidate stale research and erase its dependent records as it does in the original queues. Source/packet deletion removes dependent tasks and activity; saved presets retain unavailable scope IDs under their existing retention contract.

Validation covers actual authenticated queues, packet-versus-reviewer counts, local deadline boundaries, completed tasks, private/shared and reader/workspace access, unchanged review records, source erasure and failed component reads. Mobile browser checks exercise all three languages, counts and bounded previews, full-queue and original-packet navigation, all saved-view types, stale presets, failed-load recovery, reader privacy and source erasure.
