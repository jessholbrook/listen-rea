# Researcher PDF grounding reviews

Open **Responses → participant → Participant PDF attachment → Researcher PDF review**. After inspecting the private pages, record PDF quality, notes and, for an actually asked PDF follow-up, whether the question is supported, unsupported or uncertain. An optional researcher correction can explain a misleading interpretation or suggest better wording. A literal matching citation alone does not prove semantic support.

The panel displays the original asked question, physical page and quoted anchor. Each structured revision saves that exact probe snapshot, its fingerprint, the authenticated reviewer, timestamp, quality, grounding, notes and correction. Corrections are annotations; they never replace a participant answer or the question that was asked. Older quality-only revisions remain readable and are labeled as not having assessed grounding.

Submitted PDFs without an asked follow-up—including skipped, rejected and unavailable attempts—allow quality notes with grounding marked not applicable and no correction. Draft attachments cannot be reviewed. There is no automatic quality, authenticity or sensitive-trait score.

Reviews require the study workspace's researcher session; writes require an editing role. The API validates strict enums, 2,000-character notes, 1,000-character corrections, file/answer source, exact probe fingerprint and optimistic revision number. Stale or concurrent writes return a conflict rather than overwriting history. A changed probe requires reloading; prior snapshots remain in history. The existing file/answer fingerprint remains compatible with saved PDF quality reviews and verified passage evidence. History is bounded to 200 revisions.

Review history stays internal. The latest notes/corrections can reach separate analysis/report appendices only through [explicit inclusion](pdf-observations.md), off by default. Participant payloads, participant-theme AI inputs and external API/MCP projections do not gain these annotations. Included notes/corrections reach AI only through separate explicit [synthesis](pdf-observation-synthesis.md) or [Q&A](pdf-observation-qa.md) requests. A reviewer annotation is not a participant quotation or verified PDF passage. Existing page verification and inclusion remain separate. Private-only review edits do not change saved analyses or share links. Inclusion, edits to an included review and withdrawal invalidate them; regenerate and review before sharing again.

Deleting the source PDF removes its review history alongside source metadata and generated-question cleanup. Deleting the response removes its review documents and private files through the existing deletion queue. Existing contextual erasure also resets reviews whose source prompt was removed. Previously downloaded or transmitted copies cannot be recalled.

Validation covers legacy quality-only writes, original probe/page preservation, attributed revisions, concurrent/stale edits, strict fields, unasked/draft gates, private workspace access, participant isolation, source/response erasure and mobile reload/history. Hosted checks use a clearly marked synthetic asked-probe fixture with a real private PDF; this stage does not require an AI generation request.

[Explicitly included PDF observations in analyses and reports](pdf-observations.md) are implemented, with attribution separate from participant quotations and verified passages. [AI synthesis of included researcher PDF observations](pdf-observation-synthesis.md) is implemented. [Researcher Q&A over included PDF observations](pdf-observation-qa.md) is implemented. [Saved and reviewed PDF-observation Q&A report appendices](pdf-observation-answer-reviews.md) are implemented. Next for approval: **researcher-triggered OCR for scanned PDF pages (F12)**. OCR, broader formats and automatic scoring remain separate work.
