# Review planning and deadlines

Open a handoff packet's **Internal handoff review** panel, request reviewers, then set an optional **Review due date** for each person. Save each date explicitly. **Clear due date** empties the draft; save to remove the stored date. Editors can schedule any currently requested reviewer, including a visibly unavailable reviewer. Readers can see dates but cannot change them. English, Spanish and French controls are available. No email, reminders, domains or provider calls are involved.

Dates are coordination metadata. Changing or clearing a date preserves current content decisions, packet version, content signature and final export approval. A deadline passing never approves, withdraws or expires research. Content/context changes continue to revoke decisions through the existing review rules, while keeping planned dates. Removing a reviewer drops that person's date; readding them starts without one. This is the scheduling exception to the usual comment/reviewer/decision changes that invalidate final packet approval.

## Inbox priorities

The **Review inbox** shows dates beside each reviewer and the earliest matching unfinished review deadline for each row. Pending dates before your local calendar day are overdue; dates due today are not overdue. Approved decisions never become overdue, even when their stored date is in the past. Unavailable reviewers remain pending blockers and may be overdue.

Use the **Review deadline** filter for overdue reviews, due today, the next seven calendar days or unfinished requests without a date. The next-seven-day window includes today and the following six days, across leap days, month/year boundaries and daylight-saving changes. Deadline windows exclude completed decisions and packets with no requested reviewers. Reviewer, decision and deadline filters must match the same request; one person's approved decision cannot combine with another person's overdue date to produce a match.

Rows sort by the earliest matching pending deadline, dated requests before undated ones, then the existing blocked/awaiting/ready/exported priority, newest packet update time and packet ID. The default personal view therefore follows your dates; choosing everyone follows the earliest matching team request. Existing collection/status/title filters, 20-row pagination and missing-scope retention still apply. The overdue count counts matching review requests, not distinct packets. Packet update time describes packet content/final-review metadata, not scheduling activity. Dates and priority labels update each minute, on window focus and on refresh; a local-day change resets pagination without widening filters.

## Editing, conflicts and lifecycle

An unsaved date blocks packet editing/export, reviewer decisions, comments, reviewer-list changes and other reviewers' date edits until saved or cleared back to its original value. Saved dates use the current review version and content signature. Concurrent divergent changes return a conflict and require refresh; exact retries preserve the current version. Other sessions' dates appear when the packet/inbox refreshes. Saving a date creates no content approval or source checkpoint. Dates are omitted from packet text/CSV exports alongside other internal review coordination.

`POST /api/library/collections/:collectionId/handoff/feedback` adds strict operation `{op:"deadline",id,packetVersion,version,signature,reviewerId,dueDate}`. `dueDate` is null or a real zero-padded `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar date from 2000-01-01 through 2100-12-31, using the existing task-date validator. Only an existing editor may change dates, and the reviewer must be requested on this packet generation. Foreign users/packets, unknown reviewers, invalid dates, forged fields and stale/future writes are rejected. Scheduling increments only the review coordination version. Current packet/review and inbox views return each reviewer's current nullable due date; older records without a schedule behave as undated.

Dates are bounded to ten requested reviewers and stored in the original feedback record. No schedule jobs, copied queue documents or historical date snapshots are created. Whole packet/source/collection deletion and orphan-feedback pruning erase dates with their review. Captured context and generation checks prevent old scheduled writes from reviving removed records.
