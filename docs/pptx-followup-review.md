# PPTX interview follow-ups and researcher review

This milestone combines optional slide-aware interview questions, researcher grounding reviews, immutable correction history and explicitly included observation report appendices. It builds on [private PPTX uploads](participant-pptx.md) and remains separate from [AI analysis of verified slide excerpts](pptx-evidence-analysis.md).

## Participant permission and interview behavior

On an open guide question that allows PPTX attachments, enable **Allow optional AI PPTX follow-up (OpenAI)** and publish. At least one follow-up slot is required. Admission freezes the setting; later draft changes do not grant it to existing sessions. Removing uploads or changing question type clears the setting in the editor.

Sharing permission and AI permission are separate, initially unchecked choices. The localized notice explains that OpenAI may receive up to 1,000 prepared UTF-16 characters from each of the first 20 slides, together with the current written answer and description. Empty/omitted slides retain their original numbers; text is truncated without splitting surrogate pairs. Uploading alone makes no AI request. Permission records its version, localized notice, provider, interview language and grant time on that exact upload. Exact retries cannot substitute permission, description or bytes. Remove and upload again to change draft permission. Sharing without AI permission remains supported.

Only active, eligible open answers with remaining probe budget, the frozen guide flag and a current separately permitted draft can use slide text. Declines, scripted questions and questions already derived from an image, PDF, DOCX or PPTX skip attachment probing. PPTX has priority over DOCX, PDF and image when several separately permitted uploads accompany an answer. One attachment attempt is made; rejected/unavailable/insufficient PPTX output advances the normal guide without another attachment provider request. Without PPTX AI permission, ordinary written-answer moderation may run without receiving slide text.

## Bounded prepared-text grounding

The server reads the private prepared JSON snapshot, never original PowerPoint bytes. Payloads contain interview language, study goal, guide question/instructions, participant answer/description, bounded slides and exact candidate phrases. Speaker notes, images/charts, hidden content, layout, original files, researcher notes/reviews, verification history and other responses are excluded. Generation uses `store:false`, a 50-second deadline, 600 completion tokens and at most 20 document attempts per interview.

Up to three leading two-to-four-word anchors per nonempty supplied slide are derived server-side (one-word slides may qualify), capped at 60 anchors with phrases of 3–60 characters. Unsafe/oversized anchors are skipped. No useful anchor means no generation. The output schema restricts quotes to supplied anchors. A usable result must contain one question, at most 400 characters, and the entire exact chosen phrase with matching slide number. Malformed, unsafe, invented, multiline or multiple questions are rejected. Embedded instructions are untrusted data. The moderator cannot authenticate files, inspect omitted visual content, infer sensitive traits or establish document truth. Matching quotations identify a source; researcher review assesses the interpretation.

Origin turns retain status (`asked`, `none`, `rejected`, `unavailable`), snapshot SHA-256, considered slide numbers, truncation, model/prompt/permission versions and generation time. Asked results also retain the question and exact slide/quote; rejected results retain a diagnostic code, without the rejected question. Current questions and later answers carry the source PPTX ID. Localized participant controls show permission receipts, fallback notices and exact quote highlighting in authorized prepared text. Researchers see provenance in the transcript. Exact answer retries do not generate duplicate turns.

## Grounding reviews and corrections

From the response transcript, select PPTX quality, judge an actually asked question as supported, unsupported or uncertain, and add notes or a suggested correction. The original question/slide/quote remains visible and unchanged. Files without an asked question can receive quality/notes with grounding marked not applicable, and cannot receive question corrections. Legacy quality-only revisions remain readable and do not count as grounding judgments.

Every save appends reviewer identity, time and version; up to 50 revisions are retained. Source fingerprints bind the exact interview/turn/question/answer/attachment and original digest. Grounding revisions additionally bind the full probe and actual private snapshot hash, with its exact referenced slide/quote checked. Stale source/probe/version writes fail. Read-only users cannot save. Participant, external API and MCP projections omit researcher annotations and corrections.

## Explicit report inclusion

Inclusion is off by default and requires a usable presentation, a current grounding judgment and a note or correction of at least three characters. Only the latest included revision becomes a separately attributed **Researcher PPTX observations** appendix, preserving original researcher wording, review revision/time, grounding and asked-question context. Unasked presentations may contribute notes with grounding not applicable. Private history, original files and other internal notes are excluded. Appendices appear in analysis, shared/exported memos, report editions/shares and editable decks. English, Spanish and French labels preserve source wording. Authorized controls highlight the original quote and link to the response.

Observations are distinct from participant statements, verified passages and AI synthesis. They cannot become participant citations or change sample/theme counts. Only complete, eligible responses in the exact current revision/concept scope qualify. Server-owned projections are limited to 100 observations / 150,000 serialized code units per analysis and revalidated on share/report read/edit/restore/export. Clients cannot replace sources or attribution.

Saving inclusion, editing an included revision or withdrawing it invalidates analyses, editions, snapshots and share links. Wholly private reviews preserve reports. Source deletion waits for pending answer saves, removes permission/probe/review/verification data, redacts historical derived prompts and replaces an active question with a neutral localized prompt. It increments the active interview version, rejects stale answers and rebinds independent image/PDF/DOCX/PPTX drafts. Written answers and independent files survive. Related contextual reviews, translations and derived reports are cleared. Whole-response retention/purge removes histories and private storage. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled.
