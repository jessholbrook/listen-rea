# Reviewed attachment evidence in analysis and reports

In **Responses → participant transcript → Researcher image review**, select **Include these researcher notes in analysis and reports** and save. The setting starts unchecked, including on older reviews. Inclusion requires a usable image and at least three characters of researcher notes. Other quality flags remain internal until the reviewer marks the image usable and explicitly includes the notes.

Create a new analysis afterward. **Reviewed attachment observations** appears as a separate evidence appendix in both manual and AI analyses. It records the exact latest included notes, reviewer name/time, review revision, image ID, response/turn IDs, published revision and concept scope. Source links open the original transcript; authenticated image viewing stays private. Corrections and earlier review history are not copied into the appendix.

Only completed, included, non-preview responses qualify. The source image must still exist, belong to the same study/workspace and match the saved review fingerprint. Shared-question analyses include shared-question attachments; concept analyses include only that concept and published revision. A maximum of 100 observations and 150,000 serialized characters is supported per analysis; larger sets require reducing the included reviews.

These are researcher observations, not participant quotations or independently verified facts about an image. They do not add people to the sample, alter quantitative results or contribute to participant theme counts. AI theme coding and cited Q&A continue to use participant answers; [separate synthesis of included researcher observations](attachment-synthesis.md) is available with human review before sharing. Raw image bytes are not sent to a new model by inclusion.

The memo view shows the appendix separately from its editable narrative. **Download memo** appends it to the text export. Reviewed memo share links also include it. Report editions copy the same source-bound appendix; it is visible alongside report blocks and included in shared report text and editable PowerPoint exports. Presentation notes retain structured provenance. Report block edits cannot substitute fabricated appendix metadata. Original note wording is preserved, with English/Spanish/French attribution labels following the reporting language.

The inclusion control explains that notes and reviewer attribution appear in shared reports and exports. Public report links do not grant private image access. Participant projections and the external read API/MCP do not gain these internal metadata fields or appendices. A researcher can still write their own narrative into an existing exported/shared memo; inclusion controls govern the structured observation records.

Changing a previously included review, saving a new included review, or withdrawing inclusion clears saved study analyses, report editions/snapshots and share links. The UI discloses this effect. Regenerate the analysis and create/review a new report afterward. The underlying response and review history remain. Individual image deletion also clears derived reports even when no AI question was asked. Whole-response deletion and retention continue to erase source files, reviews and reporting data. Requests already received and downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Verification covers explicit/legacy opt-out, invalid inclusion, attribution, stable participant counts, PowerPoint/text output, forged participant citations and report metadata, participant/external projection boundaries, concept/revision and response eligibility, changed source fingerprints, withdrawal and image erasure. Chromium checks exercise mobile inclusion, persistence, source deep links, analysis/report appendices and withdrawal. Hosted checks use disposable synthetic sources, no email or AI model calls, and verify cleanup without changing existing owners or the paused pilot.
